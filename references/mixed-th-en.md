# Mixed Thai and English

Thai writers mix English into Thai all the time, especially in tech, business, marketing, and social media. Mixing is not a tell. These rules keep the rewrite from making mixed text sound less natural than it was.

## Rules

1. **Keep loanwords the writer used.** If the text says "ประชุม meeting", "ส่ง feedback", "deadline", or "แคปชัน", keep them. Do not replace them with formal Thai coinages such as "กำหนดส่ง" or "ข้อเสนอแนะ" unless the text is formal and the rest of it uses Thai terms.
2. **Do not add English the writer did not use.** Fixing TH-24 (English in brackets) means removing repeated brackets, not adding new English terms.
3. **Spacing around English words.** Thai writers usually put a space before and after an English word inside Thai text ("ส่ง feedback มาได้เลย"). Keep whatever spacing the writer uses consistently. Fix only spacing that is inconsistent within one text.
4. **Thai script for English words.** If the writer writes English words in Thai script (ทับศัพท์), such as "ฟีดแบ็ก" or "อัปเดต", keep that form. Do not switch between "update" and "อัปเดต" in one text.
5. **Patterns follow the sentence, not the word.** A Thai sentence with English words uses the Thai patterns. An English sentence inside a Thai text uses the English patterns.
6. **Side-by-side translations.** When a text has a Thai version and an English version, rewrite each in its own language with its own patterns. Keep the facts the same in both. Do not make them word-for-word translations of each other unless the source was.

## Mixed tells

### MX-1. English structure in Thai words

The Thai reads as if it was written in English first. See TH-6 to TH-10. This is common when a model writes Thai from an English prompt. Another form: English words dropped into Thai grammar more often than the team would say them ("feature login ใหม่ development เสร็จเรียบร้อยแล้ว", "รอ QA team เข้ามา test เพื่อ verify"). Keep the English words the team really uses (feature, QA, deploy, bug) and say the rest in Thai.

### MX-2. Formal Thai terms for everyday tech words

**Watch for:** "ปัญญาประดิษฐ์" where the audience says "AI", "จดหมายอิเล็กทรอนิกส์" for "อีเมล", "แอปพลิเคชันบนอุปกรณ์เคลื่อนที่" for "แอป".
**Fix:** Use the word the audience uses. Keep the formal term in government, legal, and academic text.

### MX-3. The same term in two forms

**Watch for:** "AI" in one paragraph and "ปัญญาประดิษฐ์" in the next; "แอป" and "แอปพลิเคชัน" and "application" in one post.
**Fix:** Pick the form the writer uses most and use it throughout.
